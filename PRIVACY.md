# Privacy Policy for AreUWFO
**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026

**AreUWFO** respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

### 1. Data on Your Device (Local Storage)
**We do not store your personal data on our servers.**
* **Work Logs & Settings:** Your attendance history and preferences are stored strictly on your device using local storage.
* **Deletion:** You can permanently delete this data anytime by using the "Clear Work Log" button in App Settings or by uninstalling the app.

### 2. Third-Party Data Collection
We use **Google AdMob**, **Firebase Analytics**, and **Firebase Crashlytics** to provide ads and improve app stability. These services may collect and transmit the following anonymous data:
* **Approximate Location:** Derived from IP address (for relevant advertising).
* **Device Identifiers:** Advertising ID (AAID/IDFA) and Device Type.
* **Usage & Diagnostics:** Crash logs, app launch times, and performance data.

We do not have direct access to this data; it is processed by Google under their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 3. How We Use Data
* **Local Data:** Strictly for your personal tracking within the app.
* **Third-Party Data:** To display ads (AdMob) and identify bugs (Crashlytics).
* **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share anonymous identifiers with Google AdMob for the purpose of advertising.

### 4. Your Rights & Data Deletion
* **Local Data:** You have full control to edit or delete your logs inside the app.
* **Ad Tracking:** You can opt-out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
* **Deletion Requests:** Since we do not host user accounts, we generally cannot remotely delete user data. However, for questions regarding third-party data associations, contact us at **studio.boredapps@gmail.com**.

### 5. Contact Us
For any privacy concerns, please email: **studio.boredapps@gmail.com**

*This app complies with the Apple App Store Guidelines, Google Play Developer Policy, GDPR, and CCPA requirements.*
