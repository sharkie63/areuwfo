# Privacy Policy for AreUWFO

**Last Updated:** January 25, 2026

## Introduction

AreUWFO ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

- **Work Attendance Logs**: Dates and work status (Office, Home, or Leave) that you manually enter
- **App Settings**: Your preferences including attendance goals, notification settings, and theme preferences

This data is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and is never transmitted to our servers.

### Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve app performance:
- App opens and session duration
- Feature usage statistics
- Device type and operating system version
- General location (country/region level only)

### Crash Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic data:
- Stack traces and error logs
- Device state at time of crash
- App version information

## How We Use Your Information

- **Work Logs**: Stored locally for your personal tracking purposes only
- **Analytics**: To understand how users interact with the app and improve features
- **Crash Reports**: To identify and fix bugs

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share your work attendance logs with anyone
- Use your data for advertising purposes

We DO share anonymous analytics and crash data with:
- **Google/Firebase**: For analytics and crash reporting services (subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Export Your Data**: Use the "Export to CSV" feature in Settings
- **Delete Your Data**: Use the "Clear Work Log" feature in Settings
- **Opt-out of Analytics**: Disable analytics in your device settings

## Data Retention

- **Work Logs**: Stored indefinitely on your device until you delete them
- **Analytics Data**: Retained for 14 months per Firebase's retention policy
- **Crash Reports**: Retained for 90 days

## Children's Privacy

AreUWFO is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
